# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

BikeOS is a small, proprietary project, but anyone interacting with this
repository - whether as an authorized contributor, or someone reporting a
bug or security issue - deserves to be treated with respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps keep this a positive space:
- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users (people riding
  real bicycles with this hardware attached to them).

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in
  a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainer. All reports will be reviewed and result in a response deemed
necessary and appropriate to the circumstances, up to and including
removal of access to the repository.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public
spaces.
